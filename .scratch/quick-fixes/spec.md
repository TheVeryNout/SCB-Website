# Quick fixes

Status: ready-for-agent
Branch: fix/quick-fixes
Created: 2026-11-05

Standing umbrella for tiny and small fixes (sizes as in the coding profile). Tiny fixes share a dated batch ticket `issues/NN-batch-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`; each small fix gets its own ticket in `issues/`, numbered from `01`. Work that grows past a small fix moves to its own spec and the quick-fix ticket becomes `superseded`.

A batch ends when the owner says so. Commit, push and merge each need a yes.
